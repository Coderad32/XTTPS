Experimental Secure Transport Layer

XTTPS Protocol

XTTPS is a research-oriented secure communication protocol built to explore custom certificate validation, alternative handshake design, and advanced encryption layering. XSSL is the cryptographic layer powering XTTPS sessions.

Explore Architecture Research Overview

Protocol Architecture

XSSL Layer

Custom cryptographic layer handling certificate validation, asymmetric handshake negotiation, and encrypted payload transmission.

Handshake Engine

3-stage asymmetric handshake focused on minimizing latency while maintaining session integrity and forward secrecy.

Validation Framework

Custom certificate-validation logic operating independently from traditional CA trust chains.

Security Model

Encryption

  • • ChaCha20-Poly1305 primary encryption
  • • AES-256-GCM fallback
  • • Multi-layer encryption wrapping
  • • Entropy-strength monitoring

Handshake & Key Exchange

  • • 3-stage asymmetric negotiation
  • • RSA-4096 & EdDSA certificate support
  • • Replay resistance research
  • • Experimental post-quantum padding concepts
